# Ontario tax sale listings

Data for the Ontario Tax Sale Map: open municipal tax sales in Ontario, compiled weekly from the Ontario Gazette "Sale of Land for Tax Arrears by Public Tender" notices and municipal tender packages.

- `listings.json` — `{ "updated": "YYYY-MM-DD", "listings": [...] }`. Each listing has the municipality, county, address, approximate lat/lon, roll number, PIN, legal description, assessed value, minimum tender, closing date, contact and source link.

Locations are approximate. Always read the original notice and confirm the property by PIN. For information only, not legal advice.
